# Kitchen products

Use this for food preparation, serving tools, and kitchen appliances. Prefer a
single preparation step with a visible result over a fast catalogue of features.

- Establish scale with the actual utensil, cup, or work surface.
- Show the operating step before the result: close the lid, turn the confirmed
  control, or use the tool as instructed. Preserve blade, handle, vessel, and
  ingredient continuity.
- Give pouring, slicing, stirring, or serving its own readable moment. Keep
  useful source sounds when they communicate the action.

Example concept for a storage container: place the verified contents beside it,
fill it, close its real lid, and show how it fits the intended shelf. Exact
capacity belongs in an editable overlay only when confirmed.

Do not simulate safety tests, hygiene evidence, instant cooking, or material
health benefits. Generated food and steam illustrate appearance, not actual
temperature, cooking time, flavor, or appliance performance. Check hands, tool
orientation, contact with ingredients, and consistency of the finished portion.
