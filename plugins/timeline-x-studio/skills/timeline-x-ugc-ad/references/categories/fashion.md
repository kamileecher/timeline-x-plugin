# Fashion and wearables

Use this for clothing, footwear, bags, and worn accessories. Decide whether the
viewer needs to understand fit, fabric, movement, or a functional detail.

Show the whole silhouette before a close-up. Keep the wearer, garment, color,
seams, fasteners, and accessories consistent across poses. A short turn or an
ordinary movement communicates drape more clearly than a string of reactions.
Use a factual size or material overlay only from supplied information.

Example concept for a jacket: front view, a turn showing the back, a close shot
of the actual fastening, then a neutral movement demonstrating how it sits.
The shot list records what must remain visible during a vertical crop.

Do not invent stretch, protection, medical compression benefits, universal fit,
or weather performance. Generated fabric motion illustrates styling and cannot
prove material tests. Avoid reshaping the person between shots or hiding fit
with camera angles. Voice, music, and garment sounds are optional choices based
on the intended viewing experience, not mandatory category rules.
