# Vehicle products

Use this for vehicle accessories and maintenance products. Establish the exact
compatible setting from the supplied facts; a visually similar vehicle does
not prove fit or compatibility.

Prefer a stationary vehicle and a clearly visible installation or handling
step. Keep the mount, connectors, steering controls, interior, and product
position consistent. Never add a distracting action performed while driving.

Example concept for a confirmed seat organizer: show the available mounting
points, attach it according to the provided instructions, then show ordinary
items placed in its compartments. Hold the finished fit in the same view.

Maintenance footage must follow supplied instructions and safe practice. Do not
invent fluid grades, load ratings, service intervals, fuel savings, or repair
results. A generated installation is an illustration until confirmed against
the actual product. Preserve the relevant click, fastening, or handling sound;
background music must not obscure a spoken instruction.

Inspect every attachment, product edge, and control for impossible geometry.
Keep exact model and compatibility text editable, with spelling checked against
the user's facts.
