# Style library: named looks and how Timeline X reaches them

Read this when the user names a look (a style, an era, a studio or a channel),
asks for style options, or a film needs look directions. Fifty-eight named
styles sit under the eight families in [look and light](look-and-light.md);
each row below says which Timeline X medium carries it best. A style is a
grammar of line, light, colour, type and motion, never a design to copy.

- Motion Graphics styles have full recipes: graphic and information looks in
  the Motion Graphics skill's style-recipes-graphic reference, evidence films
  and print looks in its style-recipes-evidence reference.
- Generated looks have the prompt vocabulary further down.
- 3D Studio looks (glass product, low-poly island, tilt-shift town, teardown,
  counted-object statistics, white model) follow the 3D Studio skill, with the
  vocabulary here for light and material.

## Offer a varied shortlist

When the look is open or the user asks for options, offer about six
candidates from the index: at most two from one family, a mix of media, and
none the project already used or this conversation already offered unless the
user asks again. Put the recommended one first with its reason, and keep room
for the user's own answer. When the user names a look, map it through the
names table to two or three readings and say what each would mean for this
piece. Once a style is chosen, record it in the visual bible or the Project
design style guide so later scenes keep it.

## Prompt vocabulary

Lines marked **Prompt** and **Motion** are phrase blocks for the image-generation and video-generation skills: medium,
line, light, palette, texture, then motion rate and camera. **Avoid** lists what breaks the look. **Timeline X** says
which tool carries the rest.

### cel-anime-80s
- **Prompt:** late-1980s hand-painted cel animation; flat two-tone character cels with crisp shadow and highlight shapes and coloured (never black) outlines, over a soft airbrushed backdrop with lit windows and wet reflections; signs and lamps glowing as if lit from behind the film, star flares, red-orange halation; almond eyes with layered irises, sharp-tipped hair locks; one accent colour on the lead that survives every lighting.
- **Motion:** drawings held on twos, blinks and mouth flaps even choppier; camera, flares and rain smooth; parallax slides over painted layers, slow pans across a held painted still at the peak. Each hour of day is its own colour model (indigo and magenta neon night, cobalt and coral noon, amber dusk), not a tint over the frame.
- **Avoid:** glossy modern digital anime, oversized sparkly eyes, gradients inside character shapes, uniform black vector lines, film scratches (this look is videotape), real brand names on signs.
- **Timeline X:** finish with halation, a restrained VHS or CRT pass, slight chromatic aberration and vignette; cut hard, flash white, use glitch for tracking noise; skip crossfades. Subtitles cream-yellow with a heavy dark outline (Archivo bold).

### line-boil
- **Prompt:** contemporary hand-drawn cel cartoon; each object filled flat with a base, a shade and a small highlight; coloured or dark-brown outlines that shift a hair between drawings so even a held pose breathes; small hand-drawn smoke puffs, splashes, sparks and speed lines laid over clean shapes; bright warm palette of four to six colours plus one deep ink, shadows a cooler darker shade of each base; faint paper grain.
- **Motion:** most action on twos, the quickest gesture on ones, held beats on threes; strong anticipation and overshoot, squash on landing, one stretched smear drawing at peak speed; mostly locked layout camera or a slow truck, a punch-in on the gag.
- **Avoid:** silky vector tweening, realistic anatomy, boil strong enough to look like a glitch, grey shadows, pure black lines, 1930s monochrome.
- **Timeline X:** a generation-only look; boil and smear frames cannot be added afterwards, so never fake them with jitter or glitch effects. Light grain at most; any camera shake must be in the generated motion.

### rubber-hose
- **Prompt:** early-sound-era black-and-white hand-inked cartoon; characters with even-width noodle limbs that never bend at a joint, white four-finger gloves, pie-cut pupils, huge round shoes, balloon squash and stretch; flat white, grey and ink figures over soft grey watercolour sets (patterned wallpaper, checker floors, brick); about six greys of one temperature (warm paper, cool silver or faint sepia), with the brightest whites and darkest inks kept for characters.
- **Motion:** characters on twos, beats an even number of frames so every hit lands on a drawing; every prop breathes in time with a hot jazz band; crouch, hold, snap before big moves; locked stage-like camera, medium shots so faces read, trucking pans beside runners.
- **Avoid:** any hue, elbows or knees, rendered shading, modern animation with a retro filter, distant wide shots for gags, existing cartoon characters or the cup-headed hero design.
- **Timeline X:** 4:3 gate feel via finishing: film grain, heavy vignette, slight contrast lift; transitions are circle irises, film gate slip and hard cuts on the beat, never crossfades. Title lettering: Fraunces black weight with a thick ink outline.

### scifi-toon
- **Prompt:** adult TV sci-fi sitcom cartoon; uniform thick near-black outlines with a hint of purple that boil constantly; flat fills with one hard-edged shadow shape per form, skies as flat colour bands; big white eyes with tiny pupils, heavy deadpan lids, rubbery mouths; sci-fi gadgets drawn as garage junk; a single glowing travel device whose colour appears nowhere else; each world its own three-to-five-colour palette, far from the previous one in hue and value.
- **Motion:** characters on twos, device swirl and camera smooth; acting through holds with tiny changes (twitch, gulp, sweat); squash on landings; medium two-shots, hard cuts to reaction close-ups after every absurd image, a slow push on a silent beat.
- **Avoid:** any existing show's cast, colour pairings, portal look or catchphrases; acid green with dripping bubble letters; gradients and soft glows; sparkly anime eyes.
- **Timeline X:** generate one batch per world so palettes stay coherent; leave worlds through an iris centred on the device or a hard cut, no dissolves. Captions rounded heavy white with a black outline (Bricolage Grotesque) and a speaker-coloured tag.

### midcentury-toon
- **Prompt:** 1950s flat-colour limited-animation instructional cartoon; thin warm-black ink line with small breaks, fills printed a few pixels off-register; geometric bean-headed presenter; rooms reduced to one colour plane, a floor band and a gapped horizon with one or two props; atomic sparkles, starbursts, kidney shapes, polka dots; soft complementary colours on warm cream (coral, mustard, turquoise, or tomato, olive, powder blue); dry-brush tooth, light print grain.
- **Motion:** body held still while hands, eyes and mouth move on twos; each hand action anticipates, lands on a beat and settles; camera on a stand: small slow pushes, iris through round objects, never handheld.
- **Avoid:** neon or pure colours, gradients, a bouncing black-and-white world, whiteboard-style writing, a product sitting on its own colour.
- **Timeline X:** generate presenter and backgrounds; build numbered step medallions, round call-outs, curved arrows and caption cards as Motion Graphics (Fraunces for numerals and headers, Instrument Sans for body); circle irises, colour-block wipes and beat cuts; hold the summary frame dead still for three seconds or more.

### backrooms
- **Prompt:** 1990s consumer camcorder footage, 4:3, first-person; an empty, too-clean office, hotel floor or school wing that keeps repeating; drop ceiling of humming fluorescent panels giving flat shadowless light, a few dead or flickering; one sickly dominant hue drawn from the surfaces (beige laminate, mustard wallpaper or clinic green) and exactly one saturated signal lamp; far corridors falling into murk; laser-printed numbered rule notices taped to walls; on-screen REC dot, battery and date.
- **Motion:** one continuous handheld walk with gait bob and slow breathing; autofocus hunting, exposure catching up after flickers; doors drift slowly on their own; glance down shows only shoe tips.
- **Avoid:** monsters, gore, jump scares, ruins and grime, CRT scanlines, the folklore's name, level numbers or known entities, a bright wall at the corridor end.
- **Timeline X:** join generated clips only at tape events (glitch transition as a tracking tear); finish with the VHS effect (not CRT), light grain, small chromatic aberration, vignette. Captions JetBrains Mono, white on black boxes inside the 4:3 area.

### silent-film
- **Prompt:** 1920s silent one-reeler seen as a projected print; engraved ink drawing with washed silver greys and diagonal hatching in the darks; slight sepia or one period tint per section (amber day, blue night, rose romance); flicker, weave, broken vertical scratches, dust and edge burn; 4:3 picture between velvet curtains lit by the screen; deadpan faces, bodies doing the acting; at most one element kept in colour.
- **Motion:** hand-cranked feel, motion slightly jerky and faster in chases, calmer in tender scenes; locked wide and full shots where the whole gag plays out, iris shots, tracking chases.
- **Avoid:** spoken dialogue, noir shadows as mood, a sepia filter on modern footage, the bowler-and-cane tramp look, big face close-ups, dissolves within a scene, whip pans.
- **Timeline X:** finish with grain, vignette and a warm grade; film gate slip, iris transitions and fade to black; speed changes to undercrank chases. Intertitles as Motion Graphics cards with deco borders (Fraunces for shouts, Newsreader italic for narration), a few words each.

### ukiyoe
- **Prompt:** Edo-period woodblock print filling the frame as one sheet; flat pigment areas each slightly out of register with a black carved key line of tapered, uneven width; hand-wiped gradients in sky and water; washi fibres, woodgrain and rubbing streaks visible in big colour fields; unprinted paper as the white for foam, snow and mist; one dominant blue family, red rare (seal, sun, one garment); mist as long flat bands, flat-topped pine pads, rain as fine straight lines at two angles; small figures, bold foreground crops, depth by stacked flat layers.
- **Motion:** the print stays almost still with only a detail or two in motion (rising steam, falling rain, a swaying lantern) stepping at 8 to 12 a second; the camera slides across smoothly like an unrolling scroll.
- **Avoid:** recreating a famous print (no great wave, no red mountain), vector art with a paper overlay, converging perspective, shading on forms, white paint, cross-dissolves, 3D rotation.
- **Timeline X:** generate stills and move them with slow pans and right-to-left wipes as scroll slides; title cartouches and a red seal as Motion Graphics (Newsreader).

### paper-lantern
- **Prompt:** macro photograph inside a backlit layered paper-cut shadow box; six to nine parallel sheets of cut card, each a single flat colour with fibre grain, a warm glowing rim on its upper edge and a shadowed strip at its base; rear sheets pale and translucent, front sheets nearly black; amber light glowing through the paper pulp and blazing through cut windows and moon holes; every sheet casting a soft shadow on the sheet behind it; one temperature per box plus one accent colour; shallow depth of field.
- **Motion:** paper only slides, pivots or lights up; light cues are the big events (source, then layers, then windows); short slow dollies, rack focus between layers, tiny drift on a locked frame.
- **Avoid:** a pale front layer (it flattens the depth), sculpted 3D forms, bending or squashing paper, losing the cast shadows, whip pans, digital zooms.
- **Timeline X:** generation; soft crossfades of about a second suit this look, plus iris and fade-through-dark as lights go out. Keep words as cut paper inside the picture rather than overlays.

### papercut-red
- **Prompt:** folk window-flower paper-cut brought to life; every element is opaque, front-lit cut paper whose detail comes only from what was cut away (sawtooth rows, crescents, swirls, cloud cuts, tapered slits) plus a thin inset contour inside each figure; slightly wobbly hand-cut edges, tight drop shadow; china-red hero pieces on a different paper family (cream rice paper by day, indigo dyed paper by night); depth by paper tones a step apart; gold foil saved for one late moment.
- **Motion:** jointed profile figures stepping at 12 poses a second, camera and light smooth; folds, cuts and unfolds land on beats; flat frontal views, parallax pans, top-down tabletop for cutting.
- **Avoid:** red on red, gradients, drawn strokes, painted shading, backlight as the default, closed rings that would drop out of real paper, morphing faces, dissolves.
- **Timeline X:** Motion Graphics can carry simple cut shapes, mirrored rosettes, banners and titles; generate rich scenes; transitions are wipes read as sheet peels and hard cuts. Cream Fraunces on a darker red banner strip.

### shadow-puppet
- **Prompt:** traditional shadow theatre behind a lamp-lit cotton screen; flat jointed puppets of translucent dyed hide carved like lace, colour seen through warm lamplight like stained glass, holes brightest, overlaps darker; thread rivets at joints, faint rod lines; profile figures with large headwear and contrasting sleeves; three to five warm saturated dyes plus raw hide and ink; visible cloth weave, flickering oil-lamp light, warm brown vignette; most of the cloth left empty.
- **Motion:** smooth glides with a slight hand tremor, tassels and plumes swinging; run, hop, crouch, freeze on a drum accent; audience-seat wides, pans along the screen, a short crash zoom on a hit.
- **Avoid:** pure black silhouettes, opaque paper, white or blue light (night means fewer lamps), particle fire, morphing, generic crossfades and digital wipes.
- **Timeline X:** generation; finish with warm vignette, gentle halation and light grain; transitions as fades through warm white or dark (lamps dimming) and an occasional light leak.

### hd-2d
- **Prompt:** high three-quarter view of a lit miniature 3D diorama wrapped in pixel-art textures, with flat low-res pixel sprites standing in it like cut-outs; practical lamps, fires and windows throwing real sprite shadows across the floor; fog, dust shafts or rain in the air; heavy bloom, deep vignette, a tilt-shift focus band on the hero; one broad cool or dusty ambient against small warm practicals; the hero in the one saturated costume colour; warm ink instead of black.
- **Motion:** sprites step in few-frame cycles while light, particles, focus and camera glide; the camera always looks down, on smooth splines.
- **Avoid:** voxel figures, a flat 2D-only map, eye-level views, film grain, a whole-frame pixelate filter (it destroys the lit world).
- **Timeline X:** generated stills or short clips; dialogue box with name plate and typed text as Motion Graphics (Fraunces caps, Newsreader italic, thin metal-coloured border); cut, or dip briefly to black, when changing sets.

### game-show
- **Prompt:** flat vector rhythm-game variety show; toy-like bean-bodied mascots with mitten hands, thick near-black outlines and hard offset shadows; one saturated candy background per round with a slightly lighter same-hue pattern (diagonal stripes, polka dots or a turning sunburst) over a darker floor band; no gradients, blur or texture; mascots never share the set's hue.
- **Motion:** everything on a fixed fast tempo: idle hops on the beat, squash wobble on hits, entrances dropping in on their beat, back-eased pops, frame punches; scene changes cut hard at each bar line behind a brief white flash.
- **Avoid:** soft shadows, slow fades, gradients, neighbouring rounds in the same hue, real mascots, logos or interfaces.

### microgame
- **Prompt:** home stage in flat toon with thick outline and one hard shadow, a saturated party palette around a turning sunburst, a screen-in-screen where games play, a lives board and stage badge; each game drawn in a different medium (crayon, ink wash, phosphor terminal, risograph, pixel art, blueprint, Swiss grid, clay, cut paper, woodcut) with that medium's own colours, the hero redrawn each time but keeping two or three silhouette marks and one accent dot.
- **Motion:** a shouted command, a few seconds, one action, one result; tempo, game length and drawing rate step up together every few games; failures overshoot and splat so they read.
- **Avoid:** tinting a game with the home palette, one art style throughout, failures too fast to read, full-white flashes.
- **Timeline X:** Motion Graphics for the stage, command word slam, fuse timer, lives and tick or cross stamps (Unbounded, Stack Sans Notch); generated stills per medium; pixelate, glitch or hard cuts to switch into a game.

### pixel-rpg
- **Prompt:** mid-1990s console role-playing game frame at very low native resolution scaled up with hard square pixels; limited indexed palette, ordered dither instead of smooth gradients; sprites around 20 to 34 pixels tall with a one-pixel dark outline and three-tone shading lit from the upper left; side-scrolling parallax or top-down overworld; dialogue window with a layered rounded border, dithered dark-blue fill and a face portrait.

- **Avoid:** blurry upscaling, smooth gradients or alpha fades, scanlines and grain, bloom or depth of field, existing game names, characters, interface frames or fonts.

## Weak fits for generation

- **hd-2d**: needs stepped low-res sprites inside a smoothly lit 3D set; 3D Studio is not known to render pixel sprites,
  a whole-frame pixelate ruins the lit world, and generated video tends to smooth sprite motion. Stills or very short
  clips only.
- **line-boil** (and the boil in scifi-toon and crayon-book): controlled boil, on-twos holds and single smear frames
  cannot be added in finishing, and generated clips drift toward smooth motion; results vary clip to clip.
- **pixel-rpg**: real indexed-palette behaviour (palette swaps, protected colours, stepped fades) is not available;
  posterize and pixelate only approximate it, and generated pixel art often mixes pixel sizes.
- **microgame**: a new art medium every few seconds plus an accelerating tempo ladder means many assets with one
  consistent hero; feasible but heavy, so keep it short with few games.
- **backrooms**: wants one unbroken 45 to 60 second handheld take; generated clips are short, so joins must hide behind
  tape-break glitches.
- **silent-film** and **tilt-shift**: their low-rate motion against per-frame flicker or a smooth camera has to exist in
  the source; Timeline speed changes alter speed, not the sampling rate.
- **brick-toy**: trademark risk, since generation drifts toward the real brand's figure; inspect every frame.
- **liquid-motion**: a wordmark forming exactly out of liquid is unreliable in generation, and Motion Graphics cannot be
  assumed to do metaball merging; finish the mark as a separate title.
