# Promo creative pass

Use this while planning a software promo storyboard. It does not add tools.
Drawing and placement stay on the Motion Graphics skill. Playback and export
stay on the Studio compositor.

Write this sentence before choosing any effect:

> I want to say ___ in a ___ tone, so the viewer feels ___.

One message per film. Do not invent a feature, price, customer, or statistic.

## Tone

| Tone | Rhythm | Picture | Type and colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Calm | Holds of 2–4 s, few cuts | Slow push-in | Light type, one soft accent |
| Trustworthy | Even beats | Locked frame, then one precise move | Clear sans, brand colour, neutral grey |
| Urgent | Shots of 0.4–1 s, then faster | Whip or crash zoom | Condensed type, high contrast |
| Playful | Syncopated | Tilt, small overshoot, no elastic UI | Rounded type, a second saturated colour |
| Premium | Slow, few moves | Dolly or rack focus | Serif display, dark ground, one highlight |
| Technical | On the beat, grid-locked | Orthographic, snap | Mono numbers, one signal colour |
| Warm | Slightly irregular | Subtle drift | Humanist or handwritten accent, paper tones |
| Bold | Hard cuts, big type | Frontal, large scale jumps | Heavy type, brand-colour fields |
| Mysterious | Withhold, then release | Reveal from dark | Thin type, then huge; one light |
| Celebratory | Fast payoff, then a hold | Pull back | Bold display, gold or amber accent |

Mismatches to reject: calm or premium above 1.5 cuts per second; urgent holds longer than 2 s except one pause before the payoff; trustworthy with glitch; playful with a single grey palette; more than two accent colours in one shot.

## Variety

Check the storyboard before asking for approval:

- The same transition does not sit on two consecutive cuts.
- In a film under 60 s, one transition appears at most twice unless the repeat is marked `motif:` and means the same thing each time.
- Five or more cuts use at least three families: cut, carry, camera, mask, material, time. A plain fade or push alone is not enough.
- The same headline entrance does not repeat more than twice in a row, and entrances do not all arrive from the bottom.
- Shot lengths vary. Flag the board when more than 60 percent of shots share one duration within 0.25 s.
- At least every 15 s, one shot is marked `surprise:` (scale jump, medium change, freeze, or silence).
- The accent colour leads in no more than one third of the shots.
- The film introduces one component that is not a recolored copy of a previous card.
- No run of five or more shots shares about the same length, and the pace changes at least once in every section.
- Every cut has a reason you can say in a few words: the next step, this becomes that, into or out of the subject, a jump in scale, what the subject sees, cause and effect, later, or an earlier image returning changed. Remove a cut that has none.
- Speed comes mostly from stepping one move or one interface action, not from many unrelated shots.

Timing inside a Motif uses the frame clock from the Motion Graphics skill, not an external tween package.

## Transitions

Plain hard cuts may be the quiet base. Mark a deliberate run of them `motif:cut`. Spend a narrative transition on the hook, the reveal, the proof, or the closing call. Use each named transition once in a film, and do not place two from the same family back to back.

| Family | Names | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| Cut | Shape match, motion match, colour match, beat cut with a held frame | The last frame of one shot is the first idea of the next |
| Carry | Object, word, cursor, line to horizon | One thing walks into the next shot and changes role |
| Camera | Push through, whip pan, dolly-out reveal, parallax slide | The viewer travels rather than the layers wiping |
| Mask | Product-shape mask, text mask, focal iris, split stack | A logo, word, or detail opens the next shot |
| Material | Paper fold, ink bleed, dither, glass wipe | The frame itself is the handoff |
| Time | Freeze and rewind, speed ramp, time-lapse tick, loop seam | The cut says earlier, later, or again |

## Surfaces

Score the real screen: current, calm, on-brand, and the proof is visible. High scores use the captured product as bound media, with secondary chrome dropped and type large enough to read. Low scores become a simplified Motif that shows one real job of the product. Demo numbers must read as samples. Never present them as measured customer results.
