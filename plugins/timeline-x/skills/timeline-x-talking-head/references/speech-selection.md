# Decide what the finished speech must say

Read this for retakes, highlights, a shorter edit, or a supplied target script.
Use the main skill's guarded transcription and cut path. A transcript describes
recorded content; it is not permission to fabricate a new statement.

Before selecting ranges, state the purpose and what must remain: the audience,
topic, intended length, and any mandatory sentences. An approved target script
supplies that constraint. If timing and a complete meaning cannot both fit,
preserve the meaning and report the concrete duration difference.

## Retakes and false starts

Compare complete attempts using correct meaning, intelligibility, natural
delivery, and usable picture. The last attempt is not automatically the best.
Remove the abandoned lead-in with its failed attempt while retaining the setup
needed by the chosen delivery. Listen across both sides of each proposed join.

Example: the recording says "Dosyayı önce... Pardon, önce projeyi kaydediyoruz."
Keep the complete corrected instruction. In "Yani bu dosya dışa aktarılmıyor,
projede tutuluyor," the connective may explain the correction; it is not an
automatic filler hit. Do not cut a syllable just because the transcript labels
it as a separate word.

## Target-script alignment

Map each requested idea to a complete recorded sentence or thought. Mark it as
present, a faithful spoken paraphrase, or missing. Prefer the natural matching
take. Do not silently rewrite factual terms or splice words into something the
speaker never said. A missing sentence needs a pickup recording, an explicitly
requested synthetic replacement, or an agreed omission.

If the requested order differs, move complete ideas only through currently
advertised Timeline tools and their actor guards. Recheck references such as
"bu", "sonra", and "bu nedenle" in the new context. The cut tools remove
ranges; they do not promise arbitrary sentence reordering, a separate script
file, or another editable Timeline. Resolve any unsupported assembly explicitly.

## Highlights and shorter versions

Select by the user's criterion: a conclusion, explanation, story, reaction,
or named topic. Include the question or antecedent when an answer would otherwise
be ambiguous. An opening drawn from later footage must preserve the speaker's
intended meaning; do not remove a qualification to manufacture a stronger claim.

Example: "İkinci yöntem daha iyi" needs enough context to identify both methods.
An excerpt about the second method may keep its short setup, action, and result
while dropping an unrelated anecdote. It should still stand alone.

Batch related removals, preview their effect on linked picture/audio and final
length, then apply once. Re-transcribe after timing changes. Review the finished
speech end to end against the required ideas: no lost sentence, accidental
repeat, broken connector, clipped breath, or unsynchronized linked audio.
