# Beauty and personal care

Use this for cosmetics, grooming, and personal-care products. Choose an
observable application step or material detail supported by the product brief.

- Keep packaging, applicator, product color, and the same person consistent.
- Show a small amount and a clear application action; do not invent directions,
  dosage, suitability, or an ingredient effect.
- Keep light and grading stable when showing appearance. Readable texture and
  finish are more useful than an impossible instant transformation.

Example concept for a supplied hand cream: identify the actual tube, show a
small application, then show the package beside the user's care routine. Copy
may describe a verified texture or ingredient; it cannot claim repaired skin
from the generated action.

Preserve natural skin detail. Do not frame generated before/after skin,
hair growth, medical improvement, or body changes as product evidence. Avoid
invented professional endorsements and personal testimonials. Narration can
explain a verified usage step; music should support the chosen tone without
concealing the product sounds or spoken directions.
