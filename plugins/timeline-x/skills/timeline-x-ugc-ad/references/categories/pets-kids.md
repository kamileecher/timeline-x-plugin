# Pets and children's products

Use this for pet accessories, toys, and children's products. Choose the guide
because of the use, without treating animals and children as interchangeable
performers. Product suitability must come from supplied facts.

For an accessory, show an adult preparing it, the intended supervised use, and
the product detail that explains the use. For a toy, show its actual scale and
ordinary operation. Keep small parts, contact points, age guidance, and species
or size restrictions accurate.

Example concept for a pet travel bowl: an adult opens it, adds the documented
contents, then shows it stored in the actual bag. Do not require a staged animal
reaction to make the product understandable.

Avoid distress, coercion, unsafe use, or unsupported developmental and health
claims. A generated happy reaction cannot prove preference, calming effects,
learning, or safety. Real reactions need truthful context and appropriate
permission. Preserve natural play/handling sound where useful and keep adult
instructions audible. Inspect object continuity and every contact or fastening
before describing the demonstration as complete.
