# Home and organization

Use this for storage, furniture, decor, and household appliances when the
question is how a product fits or changes a space. Route actual stain removal
to the cleaning guide and food preparation to the kitchen guide.

Keep the room, camera position, lighting, and visible inventory comparable in
a before/after. Show the rearrangement or operating step rather than making
objects disappear between cuts. Include a stable scale cue for the product.

Example concept for a shelf organizer: show the same set of items, place the
organizer, return each item, then hold the final arrangement. The viewer can
judge access and spacing without a made-up capacity claim.

For lamps or displays, preserve the setting and exposure; a dramatic grade is
not evidence of actual brightness or image quality. Quiet room tone or a light
music bed can support the demonstration without deciding the category's mood.

Check dimensions, room geometry, mounting, hinges, and material continuity.
Use a generated transformation as a clearly described concept, never as a real
installation result or a measured energy-saving claim.
