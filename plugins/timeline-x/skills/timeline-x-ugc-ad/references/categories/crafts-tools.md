# Crafts and tools

Use this for making, repair, kits, and hand or power tools. Select one material
and one operation supported by the actual product and instructions.

Show setup, the tool's contact with the material, and the result in that order.
Keep grips, blade or tip orientation, workpiece geometry, and protective setup
consistent. Let the relevant cutting, drawing, fastening, or handling sound
carry the action when source sound is available.

Example concept for a craft marker: identify the confirmed surface, show a
simple line or fill, then hold the finished detail beside the marker. A supplied
material or tip specification can appear as editable text; the demonstration
does not establish durability or suitability for every surface.

Do not invent unsafe grips, removed guards, impossible material cuts, or a
destructive comparison. Generated operation is illustrative and cannot prove
precision, strength, drying time, or performance. For a beginner kit, show one
understandable step instead of implying that a complex finished object appears
instantly. Inspect hand/tool contact and the continuity of the workpiece.
