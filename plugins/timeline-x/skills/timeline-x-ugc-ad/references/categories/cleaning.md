# Cleaning and household care

Use this for cleaning tools and supplies when the important question is what
happens on a particular surface. Establish the compatible material, method,
and relevant product instructions from supplied facts.

For actual before/after footage, keep camera, lighting, surface, and framing
consistent. Show the application or tool pass and enough of the process to
understand the result. Label time compression honestly. Do not change the stain
or surface between views to exaggerate the improvement.

Example concept for a brush: show a documented compatible surface, show the
brush movement, then hold the same area. Useful handling sounds can explain
the action; music should not bury them.

Generated cleaning footage can illustrate the method but cannot prove real
stain removal, germ reduction, pest control, or surface safety. Do not invent
chemical mixing, dosage, protection requirements, or environmental claims.
Verify these against product instructions. Inspect contact, coverage, liquid
behavior, and surface continuity; use editable factual copy rather than a
fabricated performance badge.
